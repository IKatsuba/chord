===== ? panel, 80x24
  enter to send                 ctrl + d to quit
  \ + enter for a newline       1–9 to answer a permission
  shift + enter for a newline   ↑ ↓ or j k to move between answers
  ↑ ↓ for earlier prompts       enter to pick the highlighted answer
  esc to interrupt              esc to reject a permission
  ctrl + o for verbose output   ? for shortcuts
  ctrl + c to clear input       esc to close shortcuts
  ctrl + c twice to quit
