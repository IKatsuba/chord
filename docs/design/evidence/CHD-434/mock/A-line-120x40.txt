 ⏺ With its permission option at full-access, dimcode may start other agent CLIs it finds on your PATH, such as claude,
   codex and cursor, as subagents of its own, with their approvals bypassed and on your own accounts. At
   workspace-write it asks you first, and once you allow it, the same holds. Nothing those agents do reaches chord's
   permission box. To turn a CLI off, set externalAgents.<type>.enabled to false in ~/.dimcode/v2/config.json
   (claude-code, codex, cursor, …), or use /external-agents in dimcode's own TUI. Or keep the permission option at
   read-only in /config, where dimcode refuses its agent tool.
