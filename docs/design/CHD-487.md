## The rows

- **Every subagent announced on a child session is a row while it runs**,
  claude's, codex's and cursor's, as CHD-2 draws an `agent` item.
- **The hint on an agent's or a task's row** is a monitor row's:
  `Enter for details · x to stop · Esc to close`. The shortcuts panel's
  `enter for background, details, a call` already says it.

## The detail

`enter` on an agent's or a task's row opens its detail in place of the rows.

- **The title**, as CHD-481 has it for these: `Background · 2 of 3`, no id.
- **Pinned under it, two lines** that stay while the rest scrolls, since
  the title names no item: the description, bold (the row's name: Claude's
  task, else its title; a task's title), and the state line: the row's
  state in its colour, then dim the tool uses (`1 tool use`, as the block
  counts them), for a task its kind (`task · fake · Fake Small`), and the
  owner in its colour. **The pinned name takes one line**: a name longer
  than the line is cut at its end with `…`. The state line may wrap after a
  ` · `.
- **Then the whole task** it was given, after `› ` (a monitor's command
  has `$ `), wrapped two in, never cut. When the subagent has no short
  description its name is its whole task (codex's and cursor's are named
  so); the `› ` line is then left out only if the pinned line shows the
  name whole, and kept when it was cut, so the whole task is always read.

**Why one line:** at 80×24 the frame's room is six lines; a name wrapped to
two left the work one row between its `↑` and `↓` rules, a blank row, and
ten `pageup`s did not reach the top (`subagent`, 2026-10-22). The pinned
line only has to say whose log it is; the whole task is under it, so
nothing is lost by the cut.
- **Then its work, as `ctrl+o` draws it in the block:** each message after
  `⏺`, each tool call with its `⎿` result, CHD-422's rules for output
  (whole, wrapped). A subagent of its own is drawn folded, its headline and
  `⎿` ending, as the block draws a nested one; its own work is read from
  its own row's detail.
- **Followed live.** The detail opens at its end and stays there as work
  comes, while the person has not paged up; `pageup` and `pagedown` page it
  under CHD-246's rule (`── ↑ N lines above · pageup to scroll ──`), and
  `end` comes back to the end and to following. The pinned lines and the
  hint never scroll.
- **The hints**, CHD-481's: `Enter to go to its call · ↑ ↓ for the others
  · x to stop · Esc ← to go back`.

**Why pinned, and why it opens at its end:** a monitor's detail is short
and read from the top; an agent's work is a log that grows while it is
read, and its newest line is what the person opens it for. With no id in
the title, the pinned description is what says whose log it is.
