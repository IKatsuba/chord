# CHD-525: transient prompt lists above a steady prompt

**Proposal only; owner approval of mocks, ordering and pressure policy is
required before implementation.** The owner confirmed all transient prompt
lists, including `@@`, go above the prompt. Status and permanent agents stay
below. Recommend retaining current top-first result order and keys, with
space taken from the transcript and, under pressure, a public scrolling
transient viewport. That viewport is only a source-fit candidate: no trial,
integration or compile result establishes it yet.

## Exact fixed-prompt contract

For each list, compare closed, open, and closed again at the **same viewport,
same session, same draft text/cursor/display height and same permanent
bottom content/state**. The prompt's top border and bottom border must stay
on the same rows. Keep the status/leader row below it in every state. For
completion, a closed state can be the same typed draft after dismissing its
list; do not compare a blank draft to `/` and attribute any wrap to the list.

Opening a transient list must not induce agent folding and then call the
fold an exception: that changes the permanent bottom geometry and violates
the owner's fixed-row requirement. The recommended pressure policy below
preserves that geometry. Returning from an open list to the same baseline
must restore the same border rows, not just an approximately similar cursor.

Finite comparisons that are different transitions, not evidence of this
contract:

- A recalled history match changes the displayed draft's height. Measure
  that separately, then compare SearchLine open/close with an equal-height
  recalled draft. Search itself is in scope even though its old one-row
  slot ordinarily did not grow below the prompt.
- User typing/wrapping, viewport resize, active-agent change, new permanent
  agents, agent-row wrapping, or a changed background summary alter the
  baseline. Record before/after dimensions rather than promising absolute
  fixed rows through these events.
- A question/form/link dialog replacing PromptInput is not the same box.
  Preserve that behavior and test routing, but do not claim identical prompt
  borders while the prompt does not exist.

An existing already-folded agents list remains at that same fold during a
menu-only comparison. Menu-induced folding is not covered by the exclusions.

## Pressure: the owner must choose a feasible contract

**A, recommended: preserve bottom/fold geometry; transient viewport yields.**
Transcript shrinks to zero first. Then the moved transient region uses less
visible room through public box/scrollbox sizing, existing highlight/window
state and existing scrolling keys. Long help retains its current scroll
rules. Keep selection reachable and the focused row visible; do not insert
a new paging mode or let invisible choices be silently accepted. Candidate
viewport sizing and very-small-room behavior require an unchanged-library
trial before implementation can be specified as fitting.

Current CommandList and FileList are `flexShrink={0}`; simply moving them
does not establish A. Source fit is ordinary OpenTUI composition and the
existing scrollbox/Clip, not approval to patch it, rewrite input or create a
layout engine. If the public widget cannot keep selection/overflow readable
with its existing keys at the required budget, stop and bring the concrete
mismatch and reduced-scope alternatives to the owner. This document is not
consent to a workaround.

**B: preserve existing pressure folding, relax the fixed-row contract.**
Retain [CHD-464](CHD-464.md): transcript gives way, agents fold to their floor
and stay there until transcript room returns, then request body yields.
This may move the prompt when the menu induces a fold. It explicitly relaxes
the requested guarantee and therefore requires an owner change to the task;
it cannot be selected silently for implementation convenience.

**Insufficient minimum space:** even A cannot put arbitrary pinned chrome
and a usable menu into fewer rows than their sum. The owner must approve a
reduced visible transient viewport that the public widget can support, or
relax the fixed-row guarantee/combination scope. Do not hide the problem by
cutting the permanent status, prompt, request actions or background summary.
No new rejection/modal/overlay flow is proposed without that choice.

### Source arithmetic, not a layout trial

[promptRows](../../src/ui/prompt/prompt.ts) caps **content** at 8 rows for
80 by 24 and 10 for 120 by 40. PromptInput's two border rows make a full
main prompt 10 or 12 rows respectively; its main-agent top gap is another
row. A named child has its existing separate top treatment; do not add a
second top border in a test budget.

[menuRowLimit](../../src/ui/prompt/commands.ts) is 6 at 24 high, 10 at 40.
[queueRowLimit](../../src/ui/prompt/queue.ts) and
[agentsRowLimit](../../src/ui/agents/agents.ts) are 4 and 6 respectively;
physical wrapped lines count in the agents list, not just agent count.
Shortcuts already has a public scrollbox with a minimum of zero and hidden
line rules. The transcript can reach zero; no fixed startup header row is
assumed in the following lower bound.

At 80 by 24, a full eight-content-row draft costs **11 physical rows**
including borders/top treatment and the prompt gap. Six ordinary completion
rows cost six: those list boxes have no vertical margins/borders. An
ordinary visible Working line costs two, including its top margin
([Status.tsx](../../src/ui/status/Status.tsx)); four short queued prompts
cost four; permanent Status/Leader costs one; a nonempty agents area needs
at least one physical row. This totals **at least 25 rows**, already more
than 24, even with transcript/header at zero and without plan, request,
picker or background summary. A short nonempty summary adds one; wrapped
summary text can cost more.

This is developer-reported **conditional source arithmetic**, not proof of
combined-state reachability, a full eight-content-row draft in the native
widget, or actual Yoga allocation/paint. The agents limit of four is a
budget, not a universal physical height. Even reducing agents to one does
not fit this six-result case. A needs a smaller usable transient viewport;
B's folding alone is insufficient in this example and would need a further
owner-approved relaxation. Trial the actual combined busy/queue/completion/
child state, exact margins and minimum visible controls before claiming
feasibility. No blanket success follows from arithmetic.

## Priority, focus, keys and colors

Preserve the transient slot's current precedence: search, then shared
file/agent finder, then shared command completion, then help. Move this
slot; do not turn retained states into simultaneous panels or clear them
as an invented simplification. Keep existing suppressions for bash/search,
closing and requests with keys, plus the actual ready/nonclosing gates for
already-above pickers. Do not assume global mutual exclusion from the
existing “one box” comment: helper state and an above picker may coexist.
The selected model/config picker still owns its keys; the proposal adds no
new rule that a moved completion steals them.

Keep [CHD-462](CHD-462.md)'s background/request precedence: an opened
background frame holds over already-waiting requests, while a later request
hides it until answered. Preserve retained state, request focus and the
prompt's own gates for choosing, search, agents focus and pending chords.
A pending chord still renders LeaderLine in the permanent row below.

No new action or binding. Arrows in completion still move its highlight;
Tab/Enter acceptance, Esc dismissal, Ctrl+R search behavior, `?` toggling,
help scroll/page keys, list focus and prompt cursor behavior remain as today.
Keep every existing shortcut entry and widget-owned-key test valid. Do not
reverse keys or replay them to fit option B without the owner's choice.

Use current theme roles from `src/ui/theme/theme.ts`: `permission` for
completion highlights, existing child-agent colors in `@@`, dim for
secondary rows/searching/overflow/help, prompt border and foreground as
already drawn, `accent` for Working, status mode/error roles unchanged.
No new border, color role, scrollbar style, animation or pointer action.
