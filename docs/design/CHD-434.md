# CHD-434: telling the person, once, that dimcode starts other agents

Decided by the owner (2026-10-18): when a dimcode session starts, chord
shows a one-time notice that dimcode may start other CLIs without their
approvals, and how to turn that off; the guide says the same in dimcode's
entry (writer's, later). This doc settles where it shows, what one-time
means, its words and its key.

The facts (`docs/agent-capabilities.md`, dimcode, Custom): since 0.5.19
each dimcode session looks on PATH for 18 CLIs (claude, codex, cursor's
`agent`, droid, pi-acp and others), and its model may start them as its
subagents with their approvals bypassed (claude in `bypassPermissions`, ACP
CLIs answered `allow_always`), on the person's own accounts. Nothing they
do reaches chord's permission box. No ACP switch turns it off: only
dimcode's config, or its `permission` option gating its `agent` tool, which
chord shows in `/config` as one of dimcode's options.

**Two facts the words need are not in the study:** the key in dimcode's
config that turns external agents off, and the `permission` value that
gates the `agent` tool. The mocks hold `<KEY>` and `<VALUE>` for them;
they are to be read from dimcode 0.5.19 before the build, and the words
do not go out with a guess.

## Where

### A: a warning line at the session's start, as CHD-441's

The line in CHD-441's place and form, after the settings problems and the
MCP line (`mock/A-line-80x24.txt`):

```
 ⏺ dimcode may start other agent CLIs it finds on your PATH, such as claude,
   codex and cursor, as subagents of its own. It starts them with their
   approvals bypassed, on your own accounts, and nothing they do reaches
   chord's permission box. To turn it off, set <KEY> in dimcode's own config,
   or set dimcode's permission option to <VALUE> in /config, which gates its
   agent tool.
```

- **For it:** the same place as every other "about this agent" line.
- **Against it:** a transcript line cannot be dismissed: it is text that
  scrolls. One-time with a key needs a key of its own that works from the
  prompt while the line is the newest notice, say `ctrl+x n` "not to say it
  again" (`mock/A-line-with-key-*.txt`), a global key for one notice, and
  **the shortcuts panel has no room for it at 80×24**: its 40 entries take
  20 of the screen's rows already, two columns, with the prompt box; a 41st
  makes 21 rows and pushes the panel to the screen's last line (captured
  for CHD-481, 2026-10-21). Without the key, A shows every session, which
  is not one-time.

At 120×40 (`mock/B-box-120x40.txt`) the same box, each paragraph on two

(`mock/B-after-*.txt`).

- **For it:** it is read before anything else, which is the point of a
  warning about spending and approvals; it is dismissed with keys the
  person already uses in every picker, and **no new panel line**: `1–9 to
  pick a choice`, `↑ ↓ or j k to move between choices`, `enter to pick the
  highlighted choice` and `esc to close a picker or shortcuts` cover it.
  Its widget keys go into `tests/ui/shortcuts/keys_test.tsx`, as every
  widget's do.
- **Against it:** it stops the person once before their first prompt to
  dimcode.

## What one-time means

| | Shown | For it | Against it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Per machine, until dismissed** (recommended) | the first dimcode session, then each one after until `1` is picked | the behaviour is dimcode's, wherever it runs; one answer covers it | a second project does not ask again |
| Per project | the first dimcode session in each directory | a person may care in one repository and not another | the same warning again for the same agent |
| Per dimcode version, until dismissed | as per machine, and again when chord's pinned dimcode version changes | a new version may change or end the behaviour | asks again on chord's upgrades that move the pin |
| Every session | always | never missed | not one-time; the owner asked for one-time |

**Recommended: per machine, until dismissed.** `1` records it in chord's
user directory (beside the user settings file, never the project's) and the
box is not shown again on this machine; `2` and `esc` record nothing, so it
shows at the next dimcode session. If the owner wants the version rule too,
the record holds the dimcode version it was dismissed on, and a new pin
shows it once more.

## When and to whom

- **The first dimcode session that starts while the window is open**,
  whichever it is: main, a tab, `/new`, a resume. A second dimcode session
  in the same window before the person answers does not stack a second box.
- **A dimcode child, task or team member** started by another agent: the
  box shows in the tab its permission requests reach (the parent's or the
  lead's), as a permission box would, since that is where the person looks.
- **The session runs meanwhile**: the box does not hold dimcode's start;
  it holds the keys, so typing does not reach the prompt until it is
  answered.
- **A permission or form** arriving while the box is open hides it, as it
  hides every picker (CHD-462), and the box comes back after.
- **`-p`** (no screen) says the two paragraphs once on stderr, `chord: `
  ahead, and records nothing.

## Words

- Title: `dimcode starts other agents`.
- First paragraph: `dimcode may start other agent CLIs it finds on your
  PATH, such as claude, codex and cursor, as subagents of its own. It
  starts them with their approvals bypassed, on your own accounts, and
  nothing they do reaches chord's permission box.`
- Second paragraph: `To turn it off, set <KEY> in dimcode's own config, or
  set dimcode's permission option to <VALUE> in /config, which gates its
  agent tool.`
- Choices: `1. Got it, don't show this again`, `2. Show it again next time`.
- Hint: `Enter to pick · 1–2 · ↑↓ to move · Esc to show it again next time`.

The three CLIs named are the ones a person here most likely has; the full
list is the guide's.

## Colour roles

As every picker: the border, the title and the `❯` are `permission`, the
title bold; the paragraphs plain; the hint dim. No new role.

## Keys

| Key | Does |
| --- | --- |
| `1`, or `enter` on 1 | dismiss for good on this machine |
| `2`, `enter` on 2, `esc` | close; show again at the next dimcode session |
| `↑ ↓`, `j k` | move between the two |

No new key, and no new shortcuts-panel line.

## Recommendation

**B, the box above the prompt, per machine until dismissed.** It is the one
place that is both read and dismissed with a key, and it costs the panel
nothing; A would need a global key the full panel cannot list at 80×24, or
would show every session.

## Asked of the owner

1. A or B.
2. One-time: per machine, per project, or per dimcode version.
3. Before the build, not the owner's: `<KEY>` and `<VALUE>` read from
   dimcode 0.5.19.
