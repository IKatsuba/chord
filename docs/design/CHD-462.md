# CHD-462: what opens from the summary line, instead of a full screen

The owner (2026-10-18), after living with [CHD-424](CHD-424.md): the
full-screen background view is not liked. The summary line under the agents
list stays as it is, and the items never become a list in the main UI. What
opens from that line is open: a dialog over part of the screen (the owner
suggests half the screen), or something else. The owner also asks to reuse
the pickers' frame (`src/ui/pickers/Pickers.tsx`, `borderOf`) if it fits.

These are options for the owner. Nothing is built until one is picked.
Everything else CHD-424 and [CHD-2](CHD-2.md) settle holds in each of them:
the summary line and how it is reached (`↓` from an empty prompt, `enter` on
it), the rows' four columns, their order and states, `enter` to the call, `x`
to stop at once, and clearing on the next prompt.

`ctrl+x w` and `/mcp` open, in their frame.


(`mock/A-picker-80x24.txt`; `mock/A-picker-120x40.txt` is the same frame at
full width.)

- **Where:** inline above the prompt box, full width, as every picker. The
  transcript stays above it and gives up the lines the frame takes. The
  prompt box, the status line and the summary line stay below it.
- **Size:** as tall as its rows, up to half the screen: the frame is at most
  20 lines at 40 rows (14 lines of rows) and 12 at 24 rows (6 lines of rows).
  Three items take a 9-line frame at any size. **Why half and not the
  pickers' own limit** (a quarter of the screen, 10 rows at 40): the owner
  suggested half, and a background row can wrap, so it needs more lines than
  a picker's one-line choice. At 24 rows the two are the same.
- **The frame:** `borderOf(permission)`, `paddingX={1}`, `marginTop={1}`, as
  the pickers. The title is `Background` bold in the permission colour, then
  ` · N in this tab` dim. The hint is the pickers' form, dim:
  `Enter to go to its call · x to stop · Esc to close`.
- **While it is open:** the status line says `? for shortcuts`, as it does
  under any picker, and the summary line is drawn without its `❯`, since the
  keys are in the frame. Typing does not reach the prompt.
- **A permission or form in the front tab** hides the frame until it is
  answered, as it hides every picker, and the frame comes back as it was,
  the `❯` on the same item.
- **Keys:** `↑ ↓` or `j k` move, `enter` goes to the call, `x` stops, `esc`
  closes. `enter` closes the frame and scrolls the owner's transcript as
  CHD-424 says. `esc` gives the keys back to the summary line, `❯` on it, as
  CHD-424's view does; a second `esc` goes back to the box.
- **`x`** turns the row to `■ stopped after …` in place, the `❯` on it.
  `this agent offers no stop` and CHD-2's `could not stop: …` take the hint
  line's place inside the frame until the `❯` moves.
- **Narrow:** CHD-424's rules, with 4 cells fewer for the border and its
  padding: at 80 columns the rows above fit as they are; a task's model and
  then its runtime give way first.

### What of the pickers fits, and what does not

| | Fits as it is | Does not fit, and what the frame does instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The frame | `borderOf`, the place above the prompt, the title and hint styles | — |
| Keys | `↑ ↓`, `j k`, `enter`, `esc` as `select:cancel`; the frame holds the keys | `x` is no picker key: it stays the view's, the `agents:close` action, as CHD-424 says |
| Hidden while asked | the pickers' rule, word for word | — |
| `enter` | a pick closes the picker | the pick is not a value handed back but a jump: close, pick the owner, scroll to the call |
| `esc` | closes | the pickers go back to the box; this goes back to the summary line first, as CHD-424 |
| Rows | — | `PickerList` draws each choice on one line, cut, numbered `1.`–`9.`. A background row has four columns and wraps its name (CHD-424: nothing cut), so the rows are the background view's own, inside the frame. No numbers: a digit would be a new way to jump to a call, which no answer asks for |
| Scrolling | the pickers' `↑` / `↓` gutter marks on the first and last line in view | `PickerList` scrolls by choice; a row taller than the frame (CHD-430) needs scrolling by line, so the view keeps its own scroller |
| Live updates | — | a picker's choices are a snapshot and its highlight an index. Here states and times change every second and new items join at the end, so the `❯` follows its item by id, as CHD-424 says |

So the frame and its behaviour are reused; the list inside is the background
view's, not `PickerList`.

## Recommendation

**A, the pickers' frame above the prompt.** It is where every other list in
chord opens, so the person already knows how to read it and leave it. It
sizes to what it holds, so three items cost nine lines, not half the screen.
The prompt box and the summary line stay in sight, so the summary's counts
and the rows can be read side by side, and after `enter` the call is right
above where the frame was. What it does not take from the pickers, the
one-line numbered rows and the index highlight, is what CHD-424 and CHD-430
already require of the rows.

## Colour roles

No new role, in any option. The frame's border, the title and the `❯` are
the permission role; the count, the hint line, the gutter marks and `main`
are dim; the states keep CHD-424's warning, error, success and dim; an owner
keeps its agent colour.

## The shortcuts panel

No new key in any option. CHD-424's lines hold:
`enter to open background or a call`, `x to close an agent, stop background`,
and `esc to close a picker or shortcuts`, the view counting as a picker,
which in A it is.
