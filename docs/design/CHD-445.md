# CHD-445: an agent's session title names the session in /resume and the agents list

The owner (2026-10-18): chord shows the agent's title for the session where
chord names the session today. glm, sigit, dimcode, fast-agent, minimax, pi
(after `/name`), codebuddy, qwen, codex, claude and bob send one in
`session_info_update`; auggie sends none (`docs/agent-capabilities.md` §4).
[CHD-493](CHD-493.md) dropped tabs, so the places left are `/resume` and the
start picker, the agents list, and the header of a resumed session.
CHD-491 already keeps the title (`Conversation.title`: the last update,
`null` clears it, a blank one is ignored, `/clear` resets it, a resumed
session restores it from its record) and names the terminal's status by it;
that stays as it is. Nothing the agent is sent changes, and the record's
format does not change.

Captures of today, on the fake agent, are in `evidence/CHD-445/today-*`.
The mocks below were laid out with `agentsLayout` and `wrap-ansi` at the

## What the title replaces and what it sits beside

| Where | Today | With a title |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/resume` and start picker row, main session | its first prompt | the title |
| `/resume` row, a child's session | `parser · <first prompt>` | `parser · <title>` |
| Header line of a resumed session | `resumed · 2 hours ago · <first prompt>` (a child: its name) | `resumed · 2 hours ago · <title>` (a child: `parser · <title>`) |
| Agents list, any row | `main`, `parser`, `bugfix`, `├─ tester` | the name stays; the title stands beside it in a column of its own |
| A task (`spawn_task`) | its title, in the background view and its `Agent(…)` box | unchanged **[T]** |

- **The title replaces the first prompt, never a name.** `main`, `parser`,
  `bugfix 2` and `tester` are what the person and the other agents address
  (`@parser`, `send_message`, the prompt box's `╭─ parser ─`, notifications,
  `● docs needs your permission`), so they stay wherever they are today. The
  first prompt was only ever a stand-in for what the session is about, which
  the title says better.
- **Not touched:** a child's opening line (`parser · fake · Fake Small ·
  opened by main 4m ago`) and the prompt box's border name the agent, not
  its session; the waits notice and terminal notifications name agents too.
  An adapter's subagents and monitors are not ACP sessions and have no
  title.
- **[T] Tasks** are open (sent to the lead as a policy question): the
  design keeps a task named by its `spawn_task` title alone, as its caller
  named it, and does not show the agent's own title for a task's session.
- **The title as drawn** is one line: its whitespace runs folded to one
  space, as `promptLine` folds a prompt, and its text drawn as text, an
  escape sequence in it not acted on.

row's title column is blank; when no row has a title the column is not

## The agents list

The list (CHD-471) is drawn only while the session has a child, so with no
child main's title shows on `/resume` and the terminal's status only.

- **A title column** after the name column and before the runtime and
  model: each row's title in it [text], blank for a row with none, the
  column as wide as the widest title but never wider than a third of the
  list's width, as the name column is. Two spaces either side, like every
  column. The name [agents[n]], the runtime and model [dim], the states and
  marks are as today.
- **A title wider than its column wraps inside it**, at its spaces, a word
  broken only where it alone is wider, never cut; the row's other columns
  stay on its first line and its further lines stand under the title column.
- **CHD-471's ways of fitting hold**, measured with the title column in:
  first the effort goes from every row, then the model, then a mark's words,
  then the widest row's state goes under its detail. With titles of a few
  words at 120 columns and the fake's long model name, the models usually go;
  with real models (`claude · Opus 5.5 · high`) they stay.
- **When the rows with their titles take more lines than the list's limit**
  (6 at 40 rows, 4 at 24), the title column goes from the list altogether,
  before a team folds; then CHD-471 folds and scrolls as today. A long title
  never pushes the children that wait on the person out of sight; it is
  still whole on `/resume` and in the terminal's status. The column comes
  back when the rows fit with it again.
- **A team's members** have their own titles beside their names, under the
  lead's tree; a folded lead's row carries the lead's own title.
- **A child that exits or fails** keeps the title it had; one that never
  came up has none.
- **`/clear`** resets main's title (CHD-491): its row goes blank until the
  agent names the new session.

## A title that changes mid-session

The row shows the newest title as soon as it comes (`retitled`: `Look at
the parser`, then `Fix the parser`), with no line in the transcript, no
mark and no notice: a rename is not news the person must act on. The
column may narrow or widen, and the ways of fitting are measured again, as
for any change of a row.

```
    main    Look at the parser    fake · Fake Large with a 1M context window · medium  ✻ working 2s
    main    Fix the parser        fake · Fake Large with a 1M context window · medium  ✻ working 5s
```

An open `/resume` reads its rows once, as today, so a rename while it is
open does not move them; the next `/resume` has it. The header of a resumed
session keeps what was resumed.

### A row taller than the picker

The picker never takes more lines than it does today (its rows budget, six
at 80×24), so the prompt and status line stay on screen. A row whose label
wraps to more lines than that, a title of a thousand characters for one, is
read by line, by CHD-471's rule for a row taller than the agents list,
with the picker's own keys and no new one. Nothing is cut; lines out of
sight are a press away.

- **While no tall row is highlighted**, the rows show from the top as
  today. A tall row shows as many of its lines as fit, with the gutter's `↓`
  on its last line in sight, and `… +N more` counts the rows after it.
- **Once the highlight is on a tall row**, `↑ ↓` first move through its
  lines, one line a press, and then on to the next row. The `❯` stays on
  the row's first line. Where lines or rows are out of sight, the edge
  column's `↑` marks the top line and `↓` the bottom one, as today, and no
  `… +N more` line is drawn. Every line of the row in sight takes the
  highlight colour [permission]. Enter resumes that row wherever in it the
  view is.
- **The view moves only as far as it must**: highlighting a tall row brings
  its first line into sight with the least move, so rows above stay while
  they fit. Moving off it brings the next row whole into sight the same
  way. A resize, of width or height, shows the highlighted row from its
  first line again. ctrl+a lists another set of sessions, so it opens that
  list as today: from its top, the highlight on its first row.
- **Neither of today's mechanisms does this as it is.** The picker uses no
  scrollbox. Its window (`lineRange`, after `visibleRange`) moves by whole
  rows, and puts a row taller than the window in whole, so the picker grows
  past its budget (reviewer's finding on U2). OpenTUI's scrollbox would clip
  the lines, but the view would then have to be moved by hand with
  `scrollTo` on every press. The agents list's own window, `agentsLayout`
  with `AgentsView`, already does exactly this rule in chord's code for
  the list, and the picker's window follows it.

## Error states

A title never makes an error of its own: a malformed update is ignored as
CHD-491 ignores it, and a record that cannot be read is `/resume`'s
existing `Could not read the earlier sessions: …`. A child that exits
keeps its title beside `✗ exited 2s` [error].

## Keys

None new: the title is read, not acted on. `/resume`, `ctrl+a`, `↑ ↓`,
Enter and Esc in the pickers, and `↓` into the agents list, are as today,
and the shortcuts panel is unchanged.

## Why

- **Beside the name in the list, not in place of it**: the name is an
  address the person types and the agents use; the title says what the
  session is for. Both are needed to pick the right row.
- **A column of its own, capped like the name column**: a title in the
  runtime's detail would pad every row's state to the widest title and, at
  120 columns, one long title took every row's model and mark words with
  it (tried with `agentsLayout`). The capped column keeps one long title to
  its own row's lines.
- **Titles leave the list over its limit**, before teams fold: the list is
  where the person sees who waits on them, and a title is whole elsewhere.
- **No notice on a rename**: claude and codex retitle as the work goes; a
  line each time would be noise.
