# CHD-472: dirac's tokens and cost, read as session totals

dirac sends the session's running total, from its start and across a load,
in every answer's `usage` and in `usage_update`'s cost (di20,
`docs/agent-capabilities.md`); ACP says a turn's. The owner (Q103, option
2, with main's clarification of 2026-10-21): chord shows dirac's figures as
session totals; dirac's own cost as dirac's when it sends one; when it
sends none, chord's usual estimate priced from the session's tokens, marked
as chord's estimate; no figure per turn. When dirac's provider gives no
cost, the screen says so. Drawn for the main session only (CHD-493).

## Today

`/cost` on the fake, after two prompts (`evidence/CHD-472/today/`), with a
cost reported and with tokens only:

```
 › /cost
   ⎿  Total cost:   $0.0250
      Tokens in:    2,000
      Tokens out:   400
      Cache read:   1,600
      Session time: 9s
```

```
   ⎿  Total cost:   $0.0094 (chord's estimate)
```

Every row is already the session's total, for every agent: chord adds the
turns up. On dirac it would add totals up, and grow too fast.

## /cost on dirac

**The rows and their labels stay as they are.** `Total cost`, `Tokens in`,
`Tokens out`, `Cache read`, `Cache written`, `Thinking` and `Session time`
mean the session's total for every agent, dirac's included; dirac's are its
last figures instead of a sum. **One line after `Session time` says how
they were read**, once, so no row carries a label the others lack:

```
 › /cost
   ⎿  Total cost:   $0.0250
      Tokens in:    2,000
      Tokens out:   400
      Cache read:   1,600
      Session time: 9s
      dirac reports these as the session's running total; chord shows the last it sent.
```

At 80 columns the line wraps at its spaces under its own start:

```
   ⎿  Total cost:   $0.0250
      Tokens in:    2,000
      Tokens out:   400
      Cache read:   1,600
      Session time: 9s
      dirac reports these as the session's running total; chord shows the
      last it sent.
```

- **`This turn  counted when it ends`** stays while a turn runs: it is as
  true on dirac, whose total grows when the turn's answer comes.
- **`Before …` and `Not counted`** do not arise on dirac: a running total
  has nothing earlier to add and no turn to miss.

### When dirac sends no cost

Its provider gives none. **The model has a price:** `Total cost` reads as
any estimate does, `$0.0094 (chord's estimate)`, priced once from the
session's tokens, and a second line says why:

```
      Total cost:   $0.0094 (chord's estimate)
      …
      Session time: 9s
      dirac reports these as the session's running total; chord shows the last it sent.
      dirac's provider reports no cost, so chord priced these tokens at GPT-5.3 Codex's price.
```

**The model has no price either:** the cost cell says both reasons, where
`not reported by dirac` stands today:

```
      Total cost:   not reported: dirac's provider reports none, and chord has no price for <model>
```

and the second line is left out, as the cell says it. At 80 columns the
cell wraps under its own start, past `Total cost:   `.

**Why the words:** the owner asked why there is no dirac cost; "not
reported by dirac" reads as dirac withholding it, when its provider is
what sends none.

## The spend table

With more than one session in the window, a dirac row (main, a child, a
member) reads as any other: **its cost and tokens cells are its session's
figures, as every row's are**, so no `session total` mark. Only the no-cost
case adds to its notes:

| Case | Cost cell | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| dirac sends a cost | `$0.0250` | — |
| no cost, the model has a price | `~$0.0094` | `dirac's provider reports no cost; chord's estimate from <model>'s price` |
| no cost, no price | `not reported` | `dirac's provider reports no cost; no price for <model>` |

The notes wrap inside their column (CHD-473). The window total adds a dirac
row's figures to the others', unchanged in meaning. **The Total row's note,
when a dirac cost is missing because its provider sends none, reads
`dirac's provider reports no cost`, once however many dirac rows lack one**,
in place of `dirac not reported` per row: the same reason as the rows, not
a repeat of it. Built, at 80×24 with two dirac sessions and no price, it
read `dirac not reported; dirac not reported`.

A long-lived dirac child or member is a row in that table, and its own
`/cost` when picked reads as "/cost on dirac" above.

## Unchanged

A task's `Done (… tokens · $… · 41s)` and the background view's money, `-p`
JSON, the status line: a task is one prompt in a fresh session, so its
running total is its own figure, and the others show no turn figure.
