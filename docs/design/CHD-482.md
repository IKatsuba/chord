# CHD-482: a task's end in the transcript

The owner (Q85, Q86, 2026-10-21): every ended background item leaves the
list at once, and the transcript says it ended and why, not as an error
unless it failed. [CHD-479](CHD-479.md) built that rule for agents and
monitors and wrote it for every kind ("When an item ends"); this page
applies it to `spawn_task` tasks, which were left out because a task often
says its own end, in its report, and the line must not say it twice.

Everything CHD-479 settles holds: the task leaves the summary line and the
view at once, the view's `❯` and closing follow CHD-479's "In the view", and
the line's shape is CHD-479's `<marker> <name> · <kind> · <end>`. Only what
a task adds is here. Drawn for the main session only (CHD-493).

## The line, per end

| End | Line | Colour of the marker and the end words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| done, its report came | none: the `← from … · report` message is its end line | — |
| done, no report | `✓ Fix the tokenizer · task · done in 8s` | success |
| failed | `✗ Fix the tokenizer · task · failed after 1m 12s: <reason>` | error |
| stopped with `x` in the view | `■ Watch the build · task · stopped with x after 3s` | dim |
| stopped with `esc` on its parent's turn | `■ Watch the build · task · stopped with esc after 3s` | dim |
| stopped otherwise (its agent, or closed with its owner) | `■ Watch the build · task · stopped after 3s` | dim |
| its agent's process gone | `✗ Fix the tokenizer · task · disconnected after 4m` | error |

- **The name** is the task's title, as its row and its `Agent(…)` call say
  it; **the kind** is `task`, without the runtime and model the view's kind
  carries: the line says what ended, and the call above says on what.
- **A failure's reason** is the first line of the error, through
  `theme/Ansi.tsx`, cut to the width with `…`, as CHD-479 says; with none,
  the line ends at the time.
- **The stops say who stopped it** where chord knows: `x` in the view and
  `esc` on the parent's turn are the person's, and chord sent both.
  **Why:** Q85 asks for why it ended, and a person who pressed `esc` to
  stop the parent may not have meant the task.
- **Colour:** CHD-479's: the marker and the end words in the end's colour,
  the name in the transcript's text colour, ` · task · ` dim. Only a
  failure and a lost agent are red (Q85).

## When there is no line

- **Its report says it.** A task whose last word before it ended was its
  report (`← from <title> · <runtime> · report`) and that ended done gets
  no line: the report is the line, and it says more. A task that reported
  and then failed, or was stopped, still gets its line: the report does
  not say that.
- **The turn that started it is still running** when it ends (CHD-479's
  "Not twice"): its `Agent(…)` block is the turn's live output, in front of
  the person, and already ends `Done`, `Failed` or `Cancelled`.
