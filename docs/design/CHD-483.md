# CHD-483: colour for the agents list's states, and a quieter `new reply`

The owner (2026-10-20, team `ship 6`): in the agents list under the prompt
`✻ working 11m 13s` and `◦ idle 2m` are the same dim, told apart by the
glyph alone, while the `✓ new reply` mark (CHD-471) is `success` green and
outshines every state. Decided: states get colour, at least `working`, or a
glyph that sets it apart; muted, not bright; `new reply` toned down. Open:
which colour or glyph `working` takes, whether `idle` changes, and how far
`new reply` goes down. This doc draws three variants, each built and
captured on the fake agent, in both themes.

## What the colours are today

`src/ui/agents/agents.ts` gives each state and mark a tone, and
`Agents.tsx` paints it; `/team show`'s running form paints the same tones:

| | Glyph and words | Tone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| idle | `◦ idle 2m` | dim (SGR 2, the terminal's own text) |
| working | `✻ working 11s` | dim |
| permission, input | `● permission 8s` | `warning` |
| exited, failed to start | `✗ exited` | `error` |
| mark: turn failed | `✗ turn failed` | `error` |
| mark: new reply | `✓ new reply` | `success` (#4eba65 dark, #2c7a39 light) |
| mark: new message | `← new message` | dim |

chord's palette has **no muted role**: dim is the attribute, not a colour.
So two ways are open without a new role: an existing colour drawn dim, or an
existing colour that is muted by its own value. Each variant below uses one;
a new role is weighed at the end.

## V1: working in the accent, dim; `new reply` dim

| | Dark | Light |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `✻ working` | `accent` #47e7a5 with dim | `accent` #147d55 with dim |
| `✓ new reply` | dim | dim |
| idle, new message | dim (as today) | dim (as today) |

- **Why the accent:** it is chord's colour for work going on already: the
  working line above the prompt, a running call's `⏺`, the scroll rule's
  news. A working agent in the list then reads as the same thing. Dim takes
  its brightness down, so it is muted beside the warning's and the error's
  full colour, and urgency still reads loudest.
- **`new reply` dim** is the owner's first suggestion, `new message`'s
  tone: the marks column then carries colour only for `✗ turn failed`.
- **For it:** no new role; one hue for working everywhere; the list's only
  full colours are the ones that ask for the person.
- **Against it:** how dim a colour looks is the terminal's: most halve its
  brightness, a few draw dim colour as plain colour, and there V1 is as
  bright as the accent. A reply no longer stands out at all; it is
  noticed by its `✓` and words. In the two 16-colour themes the accent is
  `yellow`, the warning's colour too, so there working and a permission
  share a hue, set apart only by dim and the glyph (`●` against `✻`); the
  build would need `accent`'s 16-colour value looked at, or working given
  another of the sixteen there. V2's glyph has the same case.

## V2: a coloured glyph, the words dim

| | Dark | Light |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `✻` of working | `accent` #47e7a5, words dim | `accent` #147d55, words dim |
| `✓` of new reply | `success` #4eba65, words dim | `success` #2c7a39, words dim |

- The glyph alone carries the state's colour, as Claude Code's own spinner
  glyph does; the words stay quiet.
- **For it:** the least colour on screen; no reliance on dim colour.
- **Against it:** in the dark theme the accent's `✻` and the success's `✓`
  are both green, a cell apart in the same row, so working and an unread
  reply read as one colour. `new reply` is quieter only by its words: its
  green spot stays.

## V3: working in the plan colour; `new reply` success, dim

| | Dark | Light |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `✻ working` | `plan` #48968c | `plan` #006b66 |
| `✓ new reply` | `success` #4eba65 with dim | `success` #2c7a39 with dim |

- `plan` is the most muted colour any theme has, a grey teal, muted by its
  value rather than by the attribute, so it looks the same in every
  terminal.
- **For it:** muted for sure; the reply keeps a trace of its green, down a
  step.
- **Against it:** `plan` means plan mode on the status line; reusing it
  for working gives a colour two meanings a line apart. Its 16-colour
  form is `cyan`, which is not muted.

## Idle

Unchanged in every variant: dim. Idle is the quiet state; once working has
a colour, the two are apart, and an idle row that draws no eye is the
point.

## Under NO_COLOR

Nothing is painted; every state is dim, as today. `working` still reads
apart from `idle` by its glyph, `✻` against `◦`
(`v1/folded-nocolor-120x40`), so the glyph need not change in any variant.

## A new muted role, weighed

A role of its own (`working`, say a desaturated green) would be a value in
six themes, dark and light with their daltonized copies and the two
16-colour ones, with CHD-87's contrast checks on black and white, and in
16 colours there is no muted green to take. What it would give is V1's look
without depending on how a terminal draws dim. Not worth it now: V1 can
take a role later if the owner finds dim colour drawn badly somewhere.

## Colour roles, in the variant built

Only `accent` (V1, V2), `plan` (V3) and `success` (V2, V3) gain a use in
the list; no role changes value and none is added. `warning` and `error`
keep their states.

## Keys

None: the list's keys do not change.

## Recommendation

**V1: working in the accent, dim; `new reply` dim.** It is the colour chord
already uses for work going on, so it adds a use and no new meaning; dim
keeps it muted beside `warning` and `error`; and dimming the reply leaves
full colour in the list only on what needs the person, which is what made
`new reply` wrong. V2 makes working and a reply the same green, and V3
borrows plan mode's colour.

## Asked of the owner

1. V1, V2 or V3, from the captures in a terminal of yours.
2. `new reply`: plain dim (V1), or a muted trace of green (V3's dim
   `success`), if V1's colours otherwise.

## Decided

The owner (Q88, 2026-10-21): **V1**, working in the accent with dim and
`new reply` dim, **with the 16-colour themes handled**, and the owner looks
at the final `.ansi` captures, dark and light, at 120×40 and 80×24, before
the merge.

**The 16-colour themes** (`dark-ansi`, `light-ansi`): their accent is
`yellow`, the warning's colour, so V1 there would draw working and a
permission in one hue. In those two themes working takes `green`
(`greenBright` in `dark-ansi`) with dim instead: the hue the accent has in
every truecolour theme, and free in the list once `new reply` is dim,
`green` being `success`, which nothing else in the list uses. The
truecolour themes take the accent as V1 says. No role changes value; the
16-colour pick is the agents list's own, beside where it reads the accent.
