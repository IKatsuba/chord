Custom shortcuts. Press ctrl+x, then a; q quits.
ctrl+x a  Complete chord
q  Quit
pageup/shift+pageup  Scroll up a page
pagedown/shift+pagedown  Scroll down a page
home/shift+home  Scroll to the top
end/shift+end  Scroll to the bottom
ctrl+x a Complete chord  q Quit  pageup/shift+pageup Scroll up a page  pagedown/shift+pagedown Scroll down a page  home…
