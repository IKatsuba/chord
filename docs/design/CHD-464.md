# CHD-464: what gives way at 80×24 when the permission box keeps its hint

The permission box is now drawn whole: its choices and its `Esc to reject`
row keep their rows (CHD-246, CHD-158 §8: a permission does not fold). At
80×24 on the fake's `teamtask`, with `tester` picked, the window's fixed
rows then add up to 25 with the transcript at none, and the last row, the
background summary line, falls off the bottom
(`evidence/CHD-464/before/teamtask-tester-80x24.txt`, the fix uncommitted,
captured by developer):

```
  ● main's task needs your permission · ctrl+x a · ⏵⏵ accept edits on    Fake…
    main       fake         ● permission 9s
    bugfix     team · fake  ◦ idle 9s
    ├─ tester  fake         ● permission 8s
    └─ coder   fake         ◦ idle 8s        ✓ new reply
```

(the `background  ● 1 task asking` line under it is cut). At 120×40 all of
it fits.

## The rule

**When the window is short, the agents list gives way after the
transcript, into its own `… +N more`.** In order, from what gives way first:

1. The transcript, down to no rows (CHD-158 §8).
2. The agents list under the status line: it shows fewer rows than its
   `max(2, ⌊rows / 6⌋)` limit (CHD-167), the rest folded into its
   `… +N more` row, down to that row alone. **The picked row stays in
   sight**, the list scrolling to it as it does at its limit, so
   `… +N more` stands for the rows around it.

Nothing else gives way: the status line, the prompt box, the queue rows,
the waiting line (CHD-158 §8), the permission box's choices and hint
(CHD-246) and the background summary line (CHD-424: wrapped, never cut)
stay whole. The list returns to its limit as soon as the rows are there
again: when the request is answered, or the window grows.

Settled by the lead (2026-10-21), as option A of developer's four: it is
what CHD-167's "at most, then `… +N more`" and CHD-424's "never cut"
imply, and it contradicts no written design. Dropping the "← from" strip
or the working line (B) and the box's blank rows (C) are named by no
design; leaving the summary line cut (D) is what CHD-424 rules out.
CHD-158 §8's order does not name the agents list; that gap is with main.
