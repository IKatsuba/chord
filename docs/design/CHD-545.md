# CHD-545: prompt-attached lists float over the transcript

The owner, 2026-10-28, and the answers to Q308–Q313 (2026-10-29): the lists
that open while editing the prompt, and the command pickers, are drawn over
the bottom of the transcript instead of taking rows from it. The transcript
keeps its height and scroll position; the prompt box and the strip below it
(status or leader line, agents list, background summary) stay on their rows
when a list opens and closes.

This replaces [CHD-525](CHD-525.md)'s budgeting of transcript rows for these
lists. Kept from CHD-525: each list's top-first order, keys and priority;
which list wins when several could show; the true-zero rule (no navigation
or acceptance while no row of a list is visible).

## What floats

- The transient slot: ctrl+r history search, `@` files, `@@` agents, slash
  commands and skills with `/new` and `/team` completion, the `?` shortcuts
  panel.
- The command pickers (Q308): `/model`, `/config`, `/resume`, `/theme`,
  `/team`, `/mcp` and the notice box.

Not floating, unchanged: the Working line, plan and todos, the queue,
permission and plan requests, the background frame, question/form/link
dialogs, the startup pickers.

## The overlay

OpenTUI draws it as it is: a box with `position='absolute'`, `bottom` and a
`zIndex` above the transcript, filled with `RGBA.defaultBackground()`, the
terminal's own background, so no cell of the transcript shows through. A
throwaway trial at 80×24 confirmed both; without the fill the text under
the list shows through every cell the list leaves blank, spaces included.

- **Bottom edge** (Q309): the row right above the prompt's gap row, so the
  list touches the prompt as today. Whatever is drawn in those rows (the
  transcript, and the Working line, plan, queue when they are there) is
  under it while it is open.
- **Top edge** (Q310, Q314): a frameless list (the transient slot) has one
  dim `─` rule across its width on top. A framed picker has no rule: its
  rounded frame is its edge. A picker's own top gap row goes.
- **Width**: the transcript's, `paddingX` 1 for the transient lists as
  today; the pickers keep their full-width rounded frame. Q311 (leave the
  scrollbar column uncovered) does not apply: chord's transcript has no
  scrollbar, and none is added.
- **Order inside the overlay**, when a picker and a transient list are open
  together: as today, the picker above, the transient list below it next to
  the prompt; no rule, the picker's frame is the top edge.

## Height

Q313: a list is as tall as it is today (`menuRowLimit`, 6 rows at 24 high
and 10 at 40; the shortcuts panel and the pickers by their own rules), but
no taller than the rows from the window's top to the prompt's gap row,
less the rule where there is one. It may cover the header. A list cut by
that room scrolls inside itself with its existing keys and keeps its
highlighted row in sight; at zero rows the true-zero rule applies.

## What is under it

- The transcript neither moves nor shrinks: same rows, same scroll, same
  following state. Its bottom rows are hidden while a list is open, and
  show again, unchanged, when it closes.
- New output while a list is open (Q312) arrives under it as it would with
  no list: a following transcript follows, a scrolled-back one stays. No
  extra mark.
- Keys keep today's routing: where a list takes a key today, it still
  does; the rest still reach the transcript.

## Command pickers

What they need that the transient lists don't:

- **They own the keys.** The prompt is not in focus while a picker is open;
  Esc closes the picker and gives the prompt back, as today. Nothing else
  about their focus or gates changes.
- **They are taller.** A frame, title, description, list and hint: `/model`
  on the fake agent is 9 rows, `/resume` and `/config` can be taller.
  Their height is capped by the same room as above; they scroll their list
  inside the frame as today, and their title and hint stay in sight. A
  framed panel with no list to shrink (`/mcp`, the notice box) cuts its
  body from the bottom instead, keeping its title and its hint or choices;
  no new scroll. A cut body drops its trailing blank row, so one gap is
  left before the hint. A picker whose room fits no choice (true-zero)
  shows its title, description and `Esc to close` alone, since its other
  keys are off. With a transient list open beside a picker, the room goes
  in origin/main's flow priority. Whatever the room, the prompt box, its
  gap row and the strip below are never moved or covered.
- **They stay open longer**, so more of the transcript is hidden for longer.
  Their frame tells the person it is a layer, and closing it
  shows the transcript as it was.

## Colour roles

No new role. The rule and the list's secondary text dim (SGR 2); highlight,
`❯` and the pickers' frames in `permission`; the overlay's fill is the
terminal's default background, which every theme, plain included, has.

## Keys

No new key or binding; the shortcuts panel needs no entry. Every list and
picker keeps its keys. Opening and closing are as today.
