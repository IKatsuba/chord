# CHD-422: tool results whole and wrapped

The owner decided (2026-10-17, option 3) that a tool call's result is
drawn whole and wrapped to the width. It is never cut in the middle and
never folded. Read keeps its first three source lines, each wrapped whole,
then the fold.
Terminal output keeps its last three lines, each wrapped. Captures are in
`evidence/CHD-422/unit/`.

`propose_team`'s view under its call is a tool result and follows the same
rule (settled by the lead, 2026-10-17). At 80 columns each role wraps under
its own column instead of being cut at the end with `…`. The body or diff
under the table is drawn whole, with no `… +N lines`. The proposal's own
dialog is unchanged.
