# CHD-405 N2: `/cost` during a turn

The owner (Q51, 2026-10-19): while a turn runs, `/cost` says the current
turn is counted when it ends, beside what the earlier turns spent; chord
guesses nothing. Codex sends a turn's spend only in the prompt's answer, and
Claude its cost only after the turn, so mid-turn `/cost` never includes the
turn that is running.

## The words

One more aligned row, **`This turn:` `counted when it ends`**:

- **after `Total cost`** when the earlier turns reported something, so it
  reads as a note on the total, and after CHD-373's `Before <date>:` and
  `Not counted:` rows when they show (CHD-378, oldest spend first);
- **after the sentence** `<agent> has not reported what this session has
  used.` on a first turn with nothing reported yet, before `Session time`.

The row is plain text, as the rows around it; `(chord's estimate)` stays on
`Total cost` where it was.

## When

**While a turn of the session `/cost` reports on is running, and nothing of
that turn's spend has arrived yet.** It is about timing, not about who
prices: a Claude turn's cost also lands only at its end, so its `/cost`
says it too. An agent that sends the running turn's spend as it goes (none
known today) has it counted, and the row goes. Once the turn ends, the row
goes and the total includes it.

**A picked child:** `/cost` reports the picked session, so the row is about
that session's own turn: tester on Codex, working under a Claude main,
says it while tester's turn runs, whatever main is doing. The same for a
member, a long-lived child and a tab.
